About Us

A Vrindavan-based public charitable trust for tree protection  and green action.

Vriksh Rakshak Dal Sewa Trust is committed to environmental conservation, tree plantation, tree protection, and social welfare activities. The Trust works primarily in Vrindavan, Mathura, and surrounding Braj regions with the vision of creating a greener, healthier, and environmentally responsible society.

Our mission is not only to plant trees but also to protect, nurture, and preserve them for future generations through public awareness and community participation.

Why Trees Matter

Nature protection is our responsibility .

Every tree supports public health, local climate, community life, and the future of the Braj region.

  • Cleaner air and healthier public spaces.
  • Better soil stability, groundwater support, and shade.
  • Protection for native biodiversity and local ecosystems.
  • Long-term sapling survival through care after plantation.
  • Greener schools, temples, ashrams, and community places.
  • A healthier environment for future generations.

One Tree. One Life. One Green Future.

Our Approach

Planting is the beginning. Protection  is the mission.

The Trust works through careful local planning, public participation, and ongoing care after every campaign.

  1. 01

    Identify Need

    Select public places, schools, temples, ashrams, and community areas that need trees.

  2. 02

    Plan Campaign

    Coordinate with local citizens, volunteers, and institutions for permissions and support.

  3. 03

    Plant Responsibly

    Choose suitable saplings and plant them with proper spacing, soil preparation, and care.

  4. 04

    Protect Saplings

    Arrange watering, guards, monitoring, and maintenance so planted trees can survive.

  5. 05

    Build Awareness

    Conduct rallies, seminars, school programs, and outreach to involve more citizens.

  6. 06

    Continue Care

    Focus on survival and long-term protection, not plantation numbers alone.
